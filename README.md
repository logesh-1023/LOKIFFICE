# LOKIFFICE v6.0 — Global Work-Life OS

## All 5 Upgrades Included in v6.0
1. **Debit-Only SMS Spend Analytics (`SmsSpendAnalyzer`):** Scans your SMS inbox locally (`content://sms/inbox`), strictly filters out all credited/refunded/salary alerts, and segregates **debited** transactions into **Commute & Fuel**, **Office Food & Cafe**, **Bills & Essentials**, and **Other Debits**.
2. **Context-Aware Workspace & Sci-Fi 3D Spatial Parking Matrix (`X, Y, Z`):** Automatically hides the "Leave Home By" calculator once your shift timer is active at the office and replaces that slot with a live rotating 3D wireframe hologram plotting your parked vehicle's `X` (lateral), `Y` (depth), and `Z` (floor height) coordinates relative to the elevator core.
3. **Interactive DND Setup Toggle (`handleDndSetupButton`):** The setup sheet button dynamically reflects whether system DND policy access is granted and always opens Android's `ACTION_NOTIFICATION_POLICY_ACCESS_SETTINGS` so you can toggle it ON or OFF anytime.
4. **Global Vehicle & Transit Support:** Removed all brand-specific scooter references in favor of universal `BluetoothClass.Device.Major.AUDIO_VIDEO` / vehicle Bluetooth and generic transit routing.
5. **Dismissible Shift-Complete Alarm (`AlarmController`):** Adds an **OK · Stop Alarm** button directly on both the lock-screen/shade notification and the in-app pulsing red banner, plus a 25-second safety auto-cutoff so the alarm never rings indefinitely.
